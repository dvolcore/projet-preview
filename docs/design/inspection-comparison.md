# Inspection comparison viewer

## Current public state

The viewer at #inspection-comparison uses existing generated pipe illustrations. The heading, prominent disclosure, image labels and alt text identify that these are illustrative conditions, not customer footage or a before/after pair from one pipe. It is outside the separate real-job proof section.

The supplied root-removal clip and its source Img_0141.mp4 show extraction at an opening. They do not establish a matched internal before/after inspection; no frames from them were relabeled as such.

## Controls

Drag the split, use Left/Right arrows, Home/End, or the preset buttons. Side-by-side retains the same buildup-left / clean-wall-right orientation and stacks on phones. The instructions change with the mode. Camera-inspection service link is a real route.

The component also supports two video elements: manual synchronized play/pause and shared seeking, bounded to the shorter duration, with no autoplay/loop. Browser tests use the existing clip twice solely as a controlled playback fixture. That fixture is never published as job evidence.

## Needed for genuine job evidence

One approved matched before/after inspection pair, with permission to publish, confirmed job/pipe/location relationship, capture order, captions and any needed redaction of customer identifiers. Replace the media and all illustration labels together only after this is supplied. Synchronizing playback does not establish that two clips show the same pipe segment.

Keep the current illustration disclosure until genuine paired media is verified. Never swap only the image paths and silently change the meaning of the comparison.
