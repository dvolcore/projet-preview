Belton housing and what it means for your pipe

The city's own data says the laterals are the problem.

Belton grew tenfold between 1950 and 1970, from 1,233 people to 12,270, then boomed again through the 1980s and 1990s. Census American Community Survey estimates put the median year built at 1987, with roughly 36 percent of the stock built between 1950 and 1979 and another 33 percent from 1980 to 1999. Two eras, two kinds of pipe.

What makes Belton unusual is how well documented the lateral problem is. The city runs an inflow and infiltration reduction program funded at around half a million dollars a year with a stated goal of cutting system inflow by 40 percent, it has budgeted to line its 27-inch and 30-inch interceptors, and its capital plan states that sanitary sewer overflows continue to be a problem. In one sewer basin study, 325 of the lateral connections examined, about 82 percent, were identified as defective.

A defective lateral is exactly what we are called about. It takes in groundwater, it lets roots in at the break, and it holds solids at the low spot the break created. The symptom at the fixture is a slow drain or a backup. The cause is forty feet away under the yard, and the only way to see it is to put a camera down the line.

Questions

Drain cleaning in Belton, answered

What does a defective sewer lateral look like from inside the house?
A slow drain, a gurgle, or a backup that returns every few months. The clue is recurrence. Belton's own basin study found about 82 percent of the lateral connections it examined were defective, so this is common here.
Is this a cleaning job or a repair job?
The camera decides. If the pipe is sound and the problem is roots, grease or scale, cleaning fixes it. If the lateral is broken or bellied, we quote the sewer line repair or replacement and tell you why.
How is hydro jetting priced?
Hydro jetting runs $450 to $800 and cable snaking runs $150 to $300, depending on line size, cleanout access, run length and what is in the pipe. You get the number for your job before we start.
