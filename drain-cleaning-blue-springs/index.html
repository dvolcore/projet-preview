Blue Springs housing and what it means for your pipe

A 1970s boom town with clay tile still in the ground.

Blue Springs quadrupled in the 1970s, from under 7,000 people in 1970 to nearly 26,000 by 1980. Roughly 28 percent of the city's housing dates to that single decade, and the Census American Community Survey puts the median year built at 1986. That means a large share of Blue Springs laterals were laid before PVC became standard residential practice.

You do not have to take our word for the pipe material. The city's own capital improvements program budgets to rehabilitate between 25,000 and 35,000 feet of clay tile sewer main every year using cured-in-place pipe, explicitly to cut infiltration and reduce the risk of backups and overflows. Clay tile joints are exactly what tree roots find. If the public main under your street is clay, the private lateral tied into it is usually the same age and the same material.

The practical difference is what a clearing job has to accomplish. On a clay or cast-iron line, a cable cuts a channel through the root mass or the grease ring and the pipe wall stays coated, so the clog rebuilds from the same spot. Hydro jetting strips the wall back to full diameter. On a genuinely one-off clog, snaking is the right call and we will say so.

Questions

Drain cleaning in Blue Springs, answered

Does Blue Springs really still have clay sewer pipe?
Yes. The city's published capital improvements program funds replacing 25,000 to 35,000 feet of clay tile sewer main a year with cured-in-place lining. Private laterals tied into those mains are usually the same vintage.
Roots keep coming back in my line every spring. Why?
Because a cable cuts a hole through the root mass without removing it or closing the joint it came in through. Jetting cuts the roots back to the pipe wall, and the camera shows whether the joint itself has failed.
Can you tell me whether I need a repair instead of a cleaning?
That is what the camera is for. If the pipe has cracked, bellied or collapsed, cleaning it is temporary, and we do sewer line repair and full replacement with excavation ourselves rather than handing you to a second contractor.
