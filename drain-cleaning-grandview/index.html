Grandview housing and what it means for your pipe

Vitrified clay from the 1960s and 70s, on the record.

Grandview grew roughly sixteenfold between 1950 and 1980, from 1,556 people to 24,502, then held almost flat for forty years. Census American Community Survey estimates put the median year built at 1973, with about 63 percent of all housing built between 1950 and 1979 and the 1960s and 1970s alone accounting for nearly half the city.

Unusually, we do not have to infer the pipe material. Engineering work done for the city describes Grandview as having about 120 miles of separate sanitary sewer, mostly vitrified clay pipe built in the 1960s and 70s, which lines up exactly with the Census housing median. Two independent sources pointing at the same answer is about as good as this kind of fact gets.

Clay fails at the joints, and joints are where roots get in. That is why Grandview has run a continuous inflow and infiltration reduction program since 2010, with documented work including over a thousand manhole inspections, nearly 250,000 feet of smoke testing and close to half a million feet of sewer cleaning and camera inspection. The public main gets that attention. Your private lateral, same age and same clay, does not until you call someone.

Questions

Drain cleaning in Grandview, answered

Is my Grandview sewer line clay?
Very likely. Engineering work for the city describes roughly 120 miles of sanitary sewer, mostly vitrified clay built in the 1960s and 70s, and Census data puts the median Grandview house at 1973. A camera confirms your line specifically.
Why do roots keep getting into a clay line?
Clay pipe is laid in short sections with joints between them, and roots follow moisture to those joints. Cutting them clears the line. Only the camera shows whether the joint itself has opened up enough to need repair.
Can you replace a collapsed section rather than the whole line?
Often, yes. We do camera inspection, sewer line repair and full replacement with excavation, so the recommendation is based on what the camera actually shows rather than on what is easiest to sell.
