Lee's Summit housing and what it means for your pipe

Mostly PVC, with mid-century pockets that are not.

Lee's Summit is the metro's long boom. It went from 2,554 people in 1950 to over 100,000 by 2020, and Census American Community Survey estimates put the median year built at 1994, with roughly 39 percent of housing from 1980 to 1999 and another 36 percent built since 2000. Under most of that is PVC.

PVC laterals do not corrode, so the slow annual decline you see in cast-iron towns is uncommon here. The failure modes we actually find in Lee's Summit subdivisions are mechanical. A line that bellied where trench backfill settled, holding water and collecting solids. Root hair that found a gasketed joint and turned into a mat. A long run with not quite enough fall, where grease from a kitchen line stops moving at the first low point. All three produce the same symptom and all three need a camera to tell apart.

The exception matters. Lee's Summit carries more than a dozen National Register historic districts, including two devoted to mid-century ranch houses, and about 21 percent of its housing was built between 1950 and 1979. Downtown and the Howard neighborhood are not PVC. On those streets we are back to cast iron and clay, and back to root cutting.

Lee's Summit owns roughly 480 miles of sanitary sewer but has no treatment plant inside the city at all. Questions

Drain cleaning in Lee's Summit, answered

Why would a 1990s house in Lee's Summit have a main line clog?
Usually a belly in the line from settled backfill, roots at a pipe joint, or grease stalling at a low spot. None of those are age-related corrosion, and all three look identical from the fixture.
Is hydro jetting safe on PVC?
Yes, used correctly. Jetting is pressurized water, not a blade, and it is the right tool for grease and root hair in a line that is structurally sound. The camera run before and after is how we confirm it is sound.
Do you work downtown and in the older ranch neighborhoods?
Yes, and we treat them differently. Downtown Lee's Summit and the mid-century districts sit on older cast iron and clay, where root cutting and full-diameter cleaning matter more than they do out in the newer subdivisions.
