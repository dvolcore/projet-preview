Lenexa housing and what it means for your pipe

A genuinely split city, and the median year hides it.

Lenexa's housing distribution is the most sharply divided we work with. Census American Community Survey estimates put the median year built at 1992, but that number is misleading, because the stock is bimodal. The two largest cohorts are the 1970s at about 20 percent and the 1980s at about 20 percent, then the 1990s drop to around 12 percent, then a second wave of roughly 37 percent arrives in the 2000s and 2010s.

On the ground that means east Lenexa, inside I-435 and around 87th Street Parkway and Old Town, is a 1970s and 1980s city, while west Lenexa out toward Renner Boulevard and the K-10 terminus is a post-2000 city. A four-decade gap in two halves of the same municipality.

The east half gives us the older pattern: cast iron inside the house, a lateral with joints that roots have had fifty years to find, and a pipe wall that has been collecting grease since the Ford administration. The west half gives us the newer pattern: bellied line from settled trench backfill, marginal fall on long runs, and root hair at gaskets in subdivisions whose trees are only now big enough to matter. Same slow drain. Different repair.

Questions

Drain cleaning in Lenexa, answered

Why does my part of Lenexa have older pipe than my coworker's?
Because Lenexa built in two waves. Roughly 40 percent of its housing went up in the 1970s and 1980s on the east side, then a second wave of about 37 percent arrived after 2000 on the west side. The eras behave differently.
What is actually causing a recurring main line clog?
On older east Lenexa pipe, usually roots at a joint or a scaled pipe wall. On newer west Lenexa pipe, usually a belly or a slope problem holding water. Recurring is the clue that the cause is still in the line.
Do you provide camera inspection, or only cleaning?
Both. Camera inspection is how we identify the cause and how we verify the line after cleaning. If the pipe itself has failed, we do sewer line repair and full replacement with excavation.
