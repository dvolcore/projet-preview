Overland Park housing and what it means for your pipe

North of I-435 and south of it are different jobs.

Overland Park incorporated in 1960 and tripled in a decade. Census American Community Survey estimates put the median year built at 1989, but the split is the useful fact: about a third of the city's housing predates 1980 and under three percent predates 1950. The pre-1980 third sits almost entirely in the older north end, inside and just outside I-435.

In Nall Hills, Oak Park, Cherokee Hills and the Metcalf corridor neighborhoods, we are working on 1960s and 1970s pipe, which means cast iron inside and clay or early plastic out, scale narrowing the line and roots at the joints. South of 135th the stock is 1990s and 2000s, and the failures are mechanical instead: bellied runs, settled backfill, root hair at gaskets, grease stalling at a low point.

Johnson County Wastewater, which handles sewer for Overland Park rather than the city, is explicit that roots in the service lateral are one of its most commonly reported customer problems, caused by cracks and breaks that come with age and soil compaction, and that the private service line belongs to the homeowner. That is exactly the stretch we clear.

Questions

Drain cleaning in Overland Park, answered

Who owns the sewer line at my Overland Park house?
Johnson County Wastewater owns the public main. The private service lateral from your building to that main is the homeowner's, and the county says roots in that lateral are among its most commonly reported problems.
Is an older north Overland Park house different from a south OP one?
Very. About a third of Overland Park housing predates 1980 and almost all of it is in the north end, on older pipe where scale and root intrusion dominate. South of 135th the usual cause is a belly or a bad slope in newer plastic.
Do you come out to Kansas, or only Missouri?
We cover the Kansas side of the metro from Grain Valley, licensed, bonded and insured, 24 hours a day including holidays. Text or call with your address and we will confirm coverage.
