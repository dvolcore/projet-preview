Raytown housing and what it means for your pipe

A 1950s city, almost nothing newer.

Raytown incorporated on 17 July 1950 and built out immediately. Census American Community Survey estimates put the median year built at 1962, the oldest of any city we serve. About 35 percent of all Raytown housing went up in the 1950s alone, nearly 73 percent between 1950 and 1979, and barely 5 percent has been built since 2000. There is effectively no PVC-era residential stock here.

That gives Raytown a very predictable drain profile: cast iron inside the house and under the slab, clay tile out to the street, and seventy years of accumulated scale and root intrusion. Cast iron does not fail suddenly. It narrows. A four-inch line with a quarter inch of scale all the way round is a three-and-a-half-inch line, and it will clog on things it handled fine a decade ago.

Raytown's own sanitary sewer division is unusually direct about this. The city maintains only the sanitary sewer main line, more than 163 miles of it with over 4,325 manholes, and it lists root control, cleaning and evaluation of each main as its standard maintenance. Root control as routine work on the public main tells you what the private laterals tied into it are dealing with.

Questions

Drain cleaning in Raytown, answered

Why does my Raytown drain clog on things it never used to?
Because the pipe is smaller than it was. Cast iron scales inward over decades, so a nominally four-inch line can be well under that. Jetting takes the wall back to full diameter instead of punching a channel through.
Does the city clean my sewer line?
No. Raytown states it maintains only the sanitary sewer main line. From your building to the main connection is the property owner's, and that is the section we clear.
Is snaking enough, or do I need jetting?
If it is a genuine one-off blockage, snaking at $150 to $300 is the right call and we will tell you so. If it keeps coming back, you are rebuilding the clog on a coated pipe wall, and jetting at $450 to $800 is what actually fixes it.
