Shawnee housing and what it means for your pipe

The oldest pipe on the Kansas side of our map.

Of the Johnson County cities, Shawnee carries the most pre-1980 housing. Census American Community Survey estimates put the median year built at 1990, but about 37 percent of the stock predates 1980 and roughly 4 percent predates 1950, both the highest shares among the four Kansas cities we cover. The city went from 845 people in 1950 to nearly 21,000 by 1970.

That early growth concentrated along Johnson Drive and the Nieman Road corridor, which is where we find the classic mid-century combination: scaled cast iron inside the house, clay or early plastic out to the main, and tree roots that have had sixty years at the joints. The west side is a different story entirely, built out through a run of annexations that ended with the last of Monticello Township coming in during 2003, which means post-1990 subdivisions on post-1990 pipe.

Knowing which half you are in changes the quote. On a scaled or root-filled older line, a cable cuts a channel and the clog rebuilds, so jetting to full diameter is usually the honest recommendation. On a newer west-side line, the question is whether the pipe has bellied or lost fall, and that is a camera answer, not a guess.

Questions

Drain cleaning in Shawnee, answered

Why is Shawnee pipe older than Olathe or Lenexa pipe?
Because Shawnee grew earlier. About 37 percent of its housing predates 1980, the highest share of the four Johnson County cities we cover, and most of that is the Johnson Drive and Nieman Road core.
My drain is slow but nothing is fully blocked. Worth calling?
Yes, and earlier is cheaper. A slow drain on older pipe usually means the line is narrowed by scale or partly rooted. Clearing it before it blocks avoids the emergency call and the cleanup.
