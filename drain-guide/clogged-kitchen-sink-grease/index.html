Clogged Kitchen Sink From Grease: Why It Comes Back

A grease clog is not something sitting in your pipe. It is bonded to the pipe wall. That one fact explains why hot water does not fix it, why chemicals do not fix it, and why snaking fixes it for a few months at a time.

The mechanism

Fats, oils and grease leave the sink as a hot liquid. Within a few feet of horizontal pipe they cool below their melting point and deposit on the wall. Every wash adds a layer, so the pipe loses diameter gradually rather than blocking at one point.

Then chemistry makes it worse. Grease reacts with calcium and magnesium in hard water and with alkaline detergent residue to form metallic soaps. It is the same saponification reaction that makes a bar of soap, and the product is a hard, waxy solid that will not re-melt when you pour hot water on it. This is why a grease clog behaves nothing like a hair clog. Hair sits in the pipe. Saponified grease is part of the pipe wall until something shears it off.

Where it builds up

In the first horizontal run past the trap arm, at the tee where the branch meets the stack, and anywhere the line has lost slope. Food solids make it denser: coffee grounds, eggshells, rice, pasta and starchy scraps do not dissolve. They embed in the grease layer and form a composite tougher than either material on its own. A garbage disposer does not change this. It makes the particles smaller and sends more of them into the same coated pipe.

Why the usual fixes fail

  • Hot water. Melts the surface of fresh grease and carries it a few feet downstream, where it cools and re-deposits in a colder, less accessible section. Saponified grease does not melt at all.
  • Boiling water and dish soap. Emulsifies the exposed face. The wall deposit stays.
  • Chemical drain cleaner. Caustic products generate heat and attack organic matter at the face of the blockage. They rarely clear a wall-bonded deposit, they can damage pipe and fixtures, and they leave standing caustic liquid for the next person who opens the line. Pro Jet does not use harsh chemicals.
  • Snaking. A cable bores a channel through the deposit. Flow comes back and the sink drains, which feels like a fix. The wall is still coated, so the channel closes again, usually faster each time because the remaining layer is thicker.

What actually removes it

Hydro jetting is the method that matches the problem. A hose with a reverse-facing nozzle is fed into the line and high-pressure water, up to 4,000 PSI, is aimed back against the pipe wall. That shears the grease and soap deposit off the wall and flushes it downstream. The line comes back to full diameter instead of a channel, which is why the interval before the next clog is measured in years rather than months.

How to tell it is grease and not the main line

  • Only the kitchen side is affected. Other fixtures drain normally, so the problem is in the branch, not the main.
  • It got slower over months rather than stopping suddenly.
  • Standing water smells sour or rancid rather than like sewage.
  • It returns on a predictable schedule after each snaking.
  • The cable comes back with white or tan waxy material on it.

If other fixtures are slow too, you are looking at a different problem. Read main line clog vs single drain clog first.

Prevention that works

Scrape plates and wipe pans into the trash. Pour cooled grease into a container and throw it away. Run cold water with the disposer, not hot, so fats stay solid and travel as particles instead of coating the pipe. Keep a strainer in the basket. And if the line has clogged twice, stop treating it as bad luck: have it cleaned properly once and then put it on an interval. See how often a drain line should be cleaned.

Commercial kitchens are the same problem at a different scale, which is why the plumbing code treats them separately. The International Plumbing Code requires drain piping upstream of a grease interceptor to slope at least a quarter inch per foot regardless of pipe size, to keep grease moving. Questions

Clogged Kitchen Sink FAQ

Will boiling water clear a grease clog?
No. It melts the surface of fresh grease and moves it downstream to cool and re-deposit. Grease that has saponified into a waxy soap does not melt at household temperatures.
Is a garbage disposer bad for my drain?
The disposer is not the problem by itself. The problem is what goes in with it. Grease, coffee grounds, eggshells and starchy scraps build a dense deposit in the branch line.
Why does my kitchen sink clog again a few months after snaking?
Because snaking opens a channel through the deposit rather than removing it. The coating on the pipe wall is still there and closes the channel again.
Can hydro jetting be used on a kitchen branch line?
Yes, where there is access to get the hose in and the slurry out. Jetting is the method that takes grease off the wall and restores full pipe diameter.
