Start with the symptom, not the service

Almost every drain problem is one of four things: a deposit on the pipe wall, an obstruction in the pipe, an air problem in the vent system, or a pipe that has lost its shape. They produce similar symptoms at the sink and need completely different work. Guessing wrong is how people pay for the same job three times.

These guides are organised the way a diagnosis actually runs. Work out which pipe is involved, then which of the four categories you are in, then decide between cleaning and repair. We do drain cleaning, hydro jetting, camera inspection and sewer line repair and full replacement with excavation, so nothing here is written to hand a job to someone else.
