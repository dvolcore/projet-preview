Hydro Jetting

How much does hydro jetting cost in Kansas City?
Hydro jetting is $450-$800 and snaking is $150-$300. Hydro jetting is full pipe wall cleaning, is long-lasting, and is best for recurring clogs, grease, roots. Snaking punches a hole through clog, is often temporary, and is best for simple, one-time clogs.
Is hydro jetting safe for old pipes?
Yes. When performed by a trained professional, hydro jetting is safe for most older plumbing systems.
How long does hydro jetting take?
Most hydro jetting jobs take between 45 and 90 minutes.
Does hydro jetting remove tree roots?
Hydro jetting can cut through small roots. Severe root intrusion may require additional repair.

Camera Inspection

Do I need a camera inspection before hydro jetting?
In many cases, yes. It helps identify the cause of the blockage and ensures jetting is the right solution.
Can a camera inspection find broken pipes?
Yes. Cracks, breaks, collapsed sections, and other structural damage.
Can you tell the exact location of a problem?
Yes. Precise depth and location data so repairs can be targeted accurately.

Drain Clearing

Why do my drains keep clogging?
Recurring clogs are usually caused by grease, hair, or buildup deeper in the line.
Are chemical drain cleaners safe?
No. They can damage pipes and rarely fix the actual blockage.
Do you clear drains for commercial properties?
Yes. Restaurants, businesses, and industrial facilities.
