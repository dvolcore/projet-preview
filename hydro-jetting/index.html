Why it works

A hole through the clog is not a clean pipe.

A cable punches a path through the blockage. Hydro jetting scours the entire pipe wall, top to bottom, so the line stays open instead of closing back up.

  • Removes grease, sludge, scale, and debris from the full pipe wall
  • Cuts through small roots and restores full pipe diameter
  • Safe for residential and commercial systems when performed by a trained professional
  • Prevents recurring clogs and backups

Problems it fixes

  • Grease buildup from kitchens and restaurants
  • Tree roots entering sewer lines
  • Recurring clogs in main drains
  • Slow drains and heavy sludge or scale in older pipes

How it works

Four steps. No surprises.

  1. 1. Camera inspection

    We confirm what is in the line and whether jetting is the right call.

  2. 2. High-pressure jetting

    Trailer-mounted jetter, up to 4,000 PSI, sized to your line.

  3. 3. Full pipe wall cleaning

    Grease, scale, sludge, and small roots come off the wall and out of the line.

  4. 4. Final verification

    The camera goes back in so you can see the clean, full-diameter line.

Hydro jetting FAQ

How much does hydro jetting cost in Kansas City?
Hydro jetting is $350-$600. Snaking is $150-$300. A camera inspection confirms what is in the line first, and we respond promptly with an estimate.
Is hydro jetting safe for old pipes?
Yes. When performed by a trained professional, hydro jetting is safe for most older plumbing systems.
How long does hydro jetting take?
Most hydro jetting jobs take between 45 and 90 minutes.
Does hydro jetting remove tree roots?
Hydro jetting can cut through small roots. Severe root intrusion may require additional repair.
