Two ways to restore flow, and what each one costs.

Hydro jetting, $450 to $800. Water up to 4,000 PSI scours grease, scale, sludge and small roots off the entire pipe wall, restoring the full diameter. Most jobs run 45 to 90 minutes.

Snaking, $150 to $300. A cable punches a hole through the blockage. Cheaper on the day, and the line is still coated, so recurring clogs tend to come back.

What moves a job toward the top of the range

  • Line size. We jet 1 inch to 12 inch and larger
  • Whether there is an accessible cleanout, or we have to pull a toilet or go through the roof
  • Length of the run and how far the truck can park from it
  • Cast iron with heavy scale, which needs descaling rather than a quick pass
  • Root intrusion, and how established it is
  • Commercial grease lines, which carry more build-up than a house line

What is included

  • The jetting itself
  • Camera verification after
  • Clean-up
  • Plain findings, not a sales pitch

What is not included, and we will say so before we start

If the camera finds a collapse, a break, offset joints or a bellied section, that is a repair and it is quoted separately. We are licensed to do that work ourselves, so you are not hunting for a second contractor, but it is not inside the jetting price and we will never pretend it is. See sewer line repair and replacement.

The honest comparison

Snaking costs less today. If the line clogs again in four months and you snake it again, you have spent more than one jetting and the pipe is still coated. If the clog is a one-off, from something that went down it that should not have, snaking is the right call and we will tell you so. A camera inspection is what decides it, not a guess.

These are real ranges, not teaser pricing. The number for your line depends on the factors above, and we quote it before any work starts.

Questions

Drain cleaning FAQ

Why do you publish prices when most plumbers will not?
Because you should be able to plan before you call. Hydro jetting is $450 to $800 and snaking is $150 to $300. The number for your line depends on size, access, length and what is in the pipe, and we quote it before we start.
Is hydro jetting worth the extra over snaking?
Usually, if the clog keeps coming back. Snaking punches a hole through the blockage and leaves the pipe wall coated. Jetting strips the wall and restores full diameter. If the clog is a genuine one-off, snaking is the right call and we will say so.
Does the price include the camera inspection?
Camera verification after the jetting is included. A full diagnostic camera run before the work, for example on a pre-purchase inspection, is a separate service.
What if you find a broken or collapsed pipe?
That is a repair, quoted separately, and we will tell you before any work starts. We are licensed to do the excavation and replacement ourselves, so you are not chasing a second contractor.
Is there an extra charge for nights, weekends or holidays?
We answer 24 hours a day, every day of the year including Thanksgiving and Christmas. Call and we will give you the number for your job before we come out.
