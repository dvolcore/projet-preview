Privacy Policy

What we collect through the quote form and contact page, and how it is used. Effective date: September 3, 2026.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Pro Jet (Pro-Jet LLC) handles information submitted through this website, including the request-a-quote form and the contact page.

Information we collect

When you submit the quote request form, we collect the information you provide: name, email address, phone number, and your message describing the service you need. Phone number is optional. We do not collect payment or financial information through this website.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, for example to call, text, or email you back with an estimate or to schedule a job. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Google Maps

The contact page includes an embedded Google Map so you can see our general service area. When that map loads, your browser loads content from Google, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that embed.

Analytics

We are adding two analytics tools to this website so we can see which pages people use and whether the phone and quote buttons work. Until they are switched on, no analytics or tracking software runs here at all.

The first is Google Analytics 4. It records page views and a small set of actions: taps on a phone number link, taps on a text message link, starting the quote form, submitting the quote form successfully, viewing a service page, and clicking a map or service area link. Google receives this data and its own privacy policy applies to it.

The second is a privacy-friendly analytics tool that counts visits without cookies and without building a profile of you across other websites.

Neither tool is used for advertising, neither is used to identify you personally, and we do not sell or share what they collect. If you would rather not be counted, most browsers offer a "do not track" or tracking protection setting, and browser extensions that block analytics scripts work normally on this site.

Cookies

This site does not set marketing or advertising cookies. Once Google Analytics 4 is switched on it will set its own cookies to count returning visits; the privacy-friendly tool described above does not. Any other cookies present are limited to what is required for the site and the quote form to function correctly.

Data retention

Quote requests are kept only as long as needed to respond to your request and complete any related work.

Your choices

You can request that we delete information you have submitted by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to this policy
