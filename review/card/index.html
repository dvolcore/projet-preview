Asking for the review

Say it once, at the end, while you are packing up

“One favor if you have a minute — scan this and leave us an honest review. If you can say what we actually did today, that is the part that helps us.”

Do

  • Hand it to every customer, every job, the same way. Not just the ones who seemed happy.
  • Ask once. If they say no or say nothing, let it go.
  • Tell them it helps to name the service: drain cleaning, hydro jetting, camera inspection, sewer line repair, line replacement.
  • If they would rather have it by text, send the link from your phone — projetkc.com/review.
  • If a customer is unhappy, fix the job. Then hand them the card like anyone else.

Never

  • Never offer a discount, cash, a free service, a gift card or anything else for a review.
  • Never make an offer conditional on the review being positive.
  • Never skip the ask because you think they will say something bad. Asking only happy customers is illegal.
  • Never write, dictate or word a review for a customer, and never write one from a Pro Jet phone or account.
  • Never ask anyone who was not actually a customer.

Why the rules are hard rules

The FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (16 CFR Part 465) makes it illegal to buy reviews, to condition an incentive on a review being positive, or to suppress honest negative reviews — including by only soliciting customers you expect to be happy. Civil penalties run per violation. Asking everyone, the same way, every time, keeps Pro Jet clear of all of it.
