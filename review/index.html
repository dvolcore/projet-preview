What makes a review useful

A couple of specifics beat five stars on their own

It helps if you mention what we did — drain cleaning, hydro jetting, the camera inspection, the sewer line repair. Anything along these lines is plenty:

  • Which service it was. Drain cleaning, hydro jetting, a sewer camera inspection, a sewer line repair, or a full line replacement.
  • Where you are. Grain Valley, Blue Springs, Independence, Lee’s Summit, Kansas City — whichever it was.
  • What was going on before we got there. Kitchen line backing up, roots in the main, basement drain flooding, whatever it was.
  • How quickly we got out to you — especially if it was a night, a weekend or a holiday.
  • Who did the work. Matt or Robert.

Those are prompts, not a script. Write it in your own words — a short, specific review in plain language is worth more than a long one.

How we ask

Honest reviews only

We ask every customer the same way, whether the visit went well or it did not, and we never pay for a review, discount a job for one, or offer anything in exchange.
