# CITED in an AI answer, and block only the two crawlers whose sole documented

# The Allow groups below are explicit rather than inherited from the wildcard.
# They do not change what any crawler may fetch; they make the intent legible
# and stop a future narrowing of the wildcard group from silently cutting off
# an answer engine.
