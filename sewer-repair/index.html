Questions

Sewer repair FAQ

How do I know if my sewer line is broken or just clogged?
A camera tells you. A clog clears and the line runs again. A broken or collapsed section shows up on screen as cracked pipe, a gap, offset joints, or standing water that will not drain. We camera the line before recommending any repair.
Do you repair the pipe, or just find the problem?
We do both. If the camera finds a collapse or a break, we excavate it, remove the failed pipe and install new pipe. You do not need a second contractor.
My house has clay tile pipe. Is that a problem?
Clay tile is common in older Kansas City homes and it is the pipe roots get into most easily. Joints separate over time and roots follow the moisture in. Jetting clears the roots. If the tile itself has cracked or collapsed, that section gets replaced.
Do you have to dig up the whole yard?
No. The camera and locator give us the exact spot and depth, so we dig where the damage is rather than opening the full run.
Can roots be cleared without replacing the pipe?
Often yes. Hydro jetting cuts roots out and restores the full diameter. Replacement is only needed when the pipe itself has failed, and the camera is what tells us which it is.
